Refunds

Last updated November 17, 2026

We want every purchase to feel worth it. If something went wrong, tell us and we'll put it right.

Who sells the sparks

Sparks are sold through Link, Stripe's checkout, as the merchant of record. Link charges your card, handles sales tax and sends your receipt, and your card statement shows LINK.COM* OVERLAND LABS. You can also ask Link support for help with a purchase, and Link may refund a purchase directly under its own terms.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@overland-labs.com from the address you use to sign in, within 30 days of the purchase. Tell us which purchase and what went wrong. We'll reply within two business days.

What we refund

  • A charge you didn't mean to make, if you haven't spent those sparks yet.
  • Double charges, always.
  • A reading or feature you paid for that didn't work. We'll return the sparks, or refund the purchase if you'd rather.

When a purchase is refunded, we remove the sparks it bought. If some of them were already spent, your balance can go below zero until you top up.

What we don't refund

  • Sparks or decks lost when you delete your own account.
  • Free welcome, gift or promotional sparks, which have no cash value.

Disputes

Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank. It's usually faster, and a dispute removes the sparks from your account while it's open.
