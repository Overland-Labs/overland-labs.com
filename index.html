Tarot and astrology, with Amber

Amber is a warm, funny guide with a 1950s soul. She pulls your card of the day, reads your stars and talks things through with you, using original illustrated decks, including seasonal decks for the holidays.

She's an AI, here for reflection and fun. She doesn't give medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

  • For adults 18 and over in the United States.
  • Works in your web browser. iPhone and Android apps come later.
  • Sign in with just your email. No password.
