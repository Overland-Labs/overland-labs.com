Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Overland Labs runs the app. This policy says what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and what control you have. The short version: we use your information to run the app for you, we never sell it, and the words you write never go into our analytics.

What we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
AccountEmail address, the first name you give, your signSign-in, and personalising readings
What you writeQuestions, chat messages, journal notesTo create readings and Amber's replies, and to keep your history
What Amber remembersShort notes Amber keeps from your conversations, for example a new job you mentionedSo Amber can pick up where you left off. You can see and delete them.
ReadingsThe cards drawn, the spread, Amber's readingYour history and journal
PurchasesSparks bought and spent, Stripe's payment referenceYour balance and receipts. Stripe handles your card; we never see the card number.
UsageScreens viewed, buttons tapped, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP addressTo understand what works and fix what doesn't. Never the text of your messages, questions or notes.
TechnicalLogs, error reports, cookies or local storageKeeping you signed in, security and fixing bugs

How we use it

To run the app, write your readings and Amber's replies, remember context you've chosen to keep, process purchases, answer support requests, keep the service safe, improve it using usage data and meet legal obligations. We don't use your information for targeted advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

AI

To create Amber's replies, we send your message, relevant history and what Amber remembers to our AI provider, Anthropic. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the app, under terms that limit their use of your data to that job:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage
  • Anthropic: AI replies
  • Stripe, through its Link checkout: payments, tax, receipts and refunds (merchant of record)
  • PostHog: product analytics
  • Expo: web hosting
  • An email delivery provider: sign-in emails
  • An error monitoring provider: crash and error reports

We also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our live systems straight away and from backups within 30 days. We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, linked to an anonymous id rather than your email.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • see and delete what Amber remembers, on the You screen
  • delete individual journal entries
  • download a copy of your data, on the You screen
  • delete your account and everything in it, on the You screen
  • turn off usage stats, on the You screen
  • email hello@overland-labs.com for anything else, including correcting your information

Depending on your state, you may have more rights, such as knowing what we hold, correcting it, deleting it, or opting out of sale or targeted advertising (we do neither). We'll answer requests within 45 days and won't treat you differently for making one. You can appeal a decision by replying to our answer.

18 and over

The app is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete an account if we learn it belongs to someone under 18.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to people who need it, and payment details stay with Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Where your data lives

In the United States.

This website

This site has no ads, no tracking and no cookies. The page fonts load from Google Fonts.

Changes

We'll post updates here and tell you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
